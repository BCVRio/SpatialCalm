Concept · Oct 2026
Resonance · Scan-to-sound · WebAR

The room
as instrument

A mobile WebAR piece for the Zappar platform. The phone's LiDAR and camera scan the user's surroundings; the point cloud, sampled light, and room palette drive a slow generative pad. The same data renders a living visual: a breathing point cloud under a particle field tinted by what the camera saw. Every session is one-of-a-kind and saved as an artefact.

Assumptions & reasoning
◆SpatialCalm repo is empty — built from scratch on the BCVR system; linked for sync once code lands.
◆Zappar WebAR — no install, runs in Safari/Chrome. Zappar's world tracking gives camera + motion; LiDAR depth comes from WebXR depth sensing where available, with a camera-based depth fallback. Permissions are therefore browser prompts, hence the explicit three-step onboarding.
◆Blue-led palette — the cool half of the gradient for depth and UI, so the warm half stays available for the Ember mood and the end-of-session artefact.
◆The prototype is live — the scan accumulates over ~14 s, "Compose" starts a real synthesised pad (turn sound on), and the visual breathes with the audio. Moods retune the chord. Sessions end into the library.
